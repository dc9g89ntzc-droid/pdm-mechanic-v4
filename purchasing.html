Scrapyard run: how to

  1. Step 1 - In the Scrapyard Dashboard, determine what quantities of each item will fit into the store's box truck. Relay those quantities into the above tally.
  2. Step 2 - Transfer the "Deposit for Import Run" total from the Shared Purchasing account to your personal account, and then deposit that same amount into the Scrapyard dashboard.
  3. Step 3 - Setup the run again in the Dashboard and go to collect the items and return them to the store.
  4. Step 4 - Transfer the "Withdraw from Shop Register" Total to your personal account and then purchase the items on the list.
  5. Step 5 - Having purchased all items, withdraw that same total from the Scrapyard Dashboard to your personal account, and then transfer that same amount to the shared purchases account.
  6. Step 6 - Select "Complete Purchase" above and bring the items back to the Mechshop for stocking.
